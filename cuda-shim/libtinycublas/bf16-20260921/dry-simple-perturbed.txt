The Thunderbolt bus carries PCI Express and DisplayPort traffic over a single cable. A host controller multiplexes the two protocols into packets, and a device controller at the far end separates them again. Bandwidth is shared, so a card that streams large buffers competes with any display on the same chain. Latency is added by the retimers, the switch inside each dock, and the protocol conversion itself.
