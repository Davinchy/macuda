# RTX 3060 (GA106, sm_86) on an M3 Max over Thunderbolt — first numbers

2026-09-17. ASUS RTX 3060 12 GB, device 0x2504 rev A1, in a Razer Core X V2, on an Apple M3 Max (48 GB, macOS 27.0).
No NVIDIA driver on the Mac. Driver is `libtinynv`'s vbios/FWSEC boot path, descriptors are QMD v3, ggml-cuda built for
`sm_86` through nvcc in a container on this same Mac.

## The gate

    sh tools/nv_shim_step.sh A opverify
    450/450 tests passed        Backend CUDA0: OK        exit 0 after 20 s

Value-checked `test-backend-ops` on the card, at the driver defaults: asynchronous submission, chain depth 128, arena
in video memory, descriptors delivered by the copy engine. The same score this tree gets on a 5090.

## llama-bench, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q4_K_M (4.36 GiB, 7.62 B), -ngl 99 -p 256 -n 128 -r 3

    | model                  |     size | params | backend | ngl |  test |             t/s |
    | qwen2 7B Q4_K - Medium | 4.36 GiB | 7.62 B | CUDA    |  99 | pp256 | 2106.19 ± 4.91  |
    | qwen2 7B Q4_K - Medium | 4.36 GiB | 7.62 B | CUDA    |  99 | tg128 |   65.11 ± 0.38  |

The whole model is resident on the card; nothing is streamed. Decode at 65.1 tok/s against a card with 360 GB/s of
memory bandwidth and a 4.36 GiB model is about 79% of what bandwidth alone allows, which is the number to beat rather
than a number to be pleased with.

## What is NOT established

A greedy decode was attempted immediately after and did not complete: the card dropped off the bus during the single
4168 MiB allocation that loads the model, which is the same enclosure power fault that dropped it twice while idle
earlier in the day. `llama-bench` loads the same model successfully, so this is marginal rather than broken, and it is
the enclosure rather than the driver - the signature is every register and every page-table entry reading all ones,
which is `dev.c`'s own description of a card that has fallen off.

So: the op gate is validated, the throughput numbers above are measured, and the byte-compared greedy decode that the
validation recipe in README.md §Run also asks for is still owed. It needs a power supply that holds under a 4 GB
transfer.
